# Independent R26 post-reveal outcome review v1

## Decision

Retain the frozen candidate for the narrow engine phrase-boundary contract. I found no required conservation, privacy, ordinary-control, primitive-bounds, or replay degradation in the frozen heldout evidence or the exposed development/legacy comparisons. This is not native controller/Undo acceptance and does not establish broad keyboard UX superiority. No known safety failure was waived for gains.

There is one novelty qualification: the decomposed `café` host prefix in `r26-h26` overlaps a later pre-freeze focused source control. All insertion-payload/right-suffix targets remain disjoint from the compared evidence. Do not describe all 40 cases as wholly lexically fresh against every source probe. Keep the original row and full denominator; report the overlap and the sensitivity result below.

This review executed no keyboard suite and changed no source, runner, expectations, frozen files, or result rows.

## Remaining misses, ambiguity, and controller limits

h30 (own lag) and h31 (queued delivery) are byte-for-byte identical baseline/candidate misses in all three modes: `moss amber.dusk` / `moss amber!dusk` rather than the aspirational spaced oracle. The conservative candidate declines to create a movable marker when initial payload delivery is unconfirmed. Those six misses remain in the full asserted denominator; neither was changed into an oracle success.

h21 (letter-apostrophe), h22 (in-word split), h27 (hidden-before), and h29 (hidden-both) remain explicitly ambiguous, unchanged outputs. They do not claim semantic gains. Hidden contexts do not acquire fabricated right boundaries. Existing right whitespace, newline, punctuation and emoji, numeric/code/emoji payloads, shared whitespace authority, shared left-only insertion, and literal raw insertion remain unchanged controls. Private, URL and numeric policies create no phrase separator. The additional ASCII-folded-letter restriction remains a conservative script-coverage limit; this heldout does not independently establish support for other scripts.

h12 and h40 are controller-route failures with zero document primitives in both versions. Their selected/private destinations are not accepted transcript destinations under the current production controller guards; direct engine selected insertion in h05 does not demonstrate controller delivery. Automatic delivery requires the original continuously observed identified visible non-private no-selection destination. Recovery confirmation also refuses private/selected destinations; hidden confirmation and automatic delivery are different routes. No engine substitution silently turns these controller rows into passes.

h39 supplies only a nonasserting pre-Undo reference document. It remains a full-denominator unavailable failure even though its candidate document matches that reference. Backspace h13, abstract primitive replay, and UTF-16 caret recording prove no native Undo grouping or cursor-delivery behavior. There was no UIKit/device execution.

The earlier source review's mid-run ownership limitation remains: earlier confirmed owned-marker snapshots can preserve an uninterrupted edit stream under proxy lag, but a physical host transition hidden behind an identical stale snapshot cannot be proven safe from this API alone. No required frozen safety failure is excused by that limitation. Five pre-existing known core issues remain disclosed in the ordinary gate evidence; this result does not claim to solve them.

## Independent lexical novelty check

`novelty-review-v1.json` records the comparison identities, token sets, overlaps, and sensitivity calculation. Normalization is NFC, Unicode casefold, NFC, followed by maximal Unicode general-category L/M/N runs. Heldout lexical content uses original prefix/selected/suffix and action text; metadata and derived expected-string fusions are excluded. Comparator examples include expected text conservatively. I checked all exposed old R5/R20 development/source-control/former-heldout pairs, the 456-entry frozen exclusion list, new R26 development cases, focused Swift source-control literals, and copied reviewer-authored source-probe suites.

The heldout has 19 distinct content tokens. All are disjoint from old R5/R20, the frozen exclusions, and new R26 development content. The only overlap against later focused source controls is h26's host prefix `cafe\u{301}` → `café`. It does not overlap an insertion payload or right-suffix target. The sealed author could not have used later source-control results, but strict full-content disjointness against all final pre-freeze probes is still false and must be disclosed.

Without changing or excluding any original reported row, a sensitivity calculation omitting h26's three repeated-mode executions gives baseline 48 → candidate 90 exact out of 117 full executions (102 asserted, 15 ambiguous, nine unavailable), with 42 gains and zero regressions. Primary results remain the original 120-execution matrix. This qualification reduces the lexical novelty claim; it does not justify an oracle edit, rerun, safety waiver, or a broader native-acceptance claim.
