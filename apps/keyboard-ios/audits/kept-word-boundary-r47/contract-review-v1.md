## 1. Sparse alignment: admit the new premise

`SpellCorrector.swift:4–37` establishes positional optional slots. `append` retains nil entries; `keyed` associates each Character with its slot, then normalizes that Character. `TouchModel.swift:110–141` skips nils and uses the existing target/keyed length and geometry rules. Thus own `ca` plus own nil-tap `noe` has five aligned slots and two available points. In a qualifying fresh, unsaturated `canoe` acceptance, those two points may teach `c/a`; the absent positions teach nothing. This is a newly declared R47 premise, never a repair of R44 case64.

Inherited `mu` followed by tapped `sic` has only three slots for five Characters. It remains excluded. Padding that inherited prefix now, inferring origin from equal counts, or using the ordinary Space result as an ownership certificate would evade the intended rule. Conversely an all-nonnil guard would discard supported input: current Controller grid activation supplies a point, while `insertAlternate` calls `insertCharacter(text)` without one (`KeyboardController.swift:1107–1144`). Native alternate input must retain nil at its actual position and preserve the other aligned points.

“Available samples” must mean the samples actually supported by the unchanged keyed/target/geometry mapping, not every Character or every nonnil slot indiscriminately. Apostrophes can disappear in normalization; unsupported mappings do not acquire invented coordinates. Keep precomposed/decomposed Spanish paths separate unless their mapping is independently established. No new general Unicode mapping algorithm is required.

## 2. Blocking clarification: precise deletion ownership rule

The draft's “provided current live document/selection still match” needs to say **both before and after the deletion primitive**. Current default `deleteBackward` requests document deletion and then calls `composing.removeLast()` without a success result (`KeyboardEngine.swift:522–573`). Array shortening alone cannot certify which text the host removed.

Freeze this rule:

> An already owned, unaccepted occurrence W may retain ownership after an ordinary single-Character suffix backspace only when a complete live pre-snapshot proves collapsed selection at W's end, the same occurrence/document identity, and aligned slots; and a live synchronous post-snapshot proves exactly the old prefix minus W's last Character, the identical suffix, collapsed selection, unchanged observable identity, and no intervening ownership invalidation. Remove exactly that Character's slot, including a nil slot. The retained nonempty prefix keeps the occurrence's ownership. Its spelling need not already be valid during editing; lexical eligibility is checked at acceptance. Subsequent own appends must preserve alignment and continuity. Deletion to empty ends the occurrence.

> An undo/restoration branch, selection deletion, derived word after separator deletion, whole-word deletion/undo, host replacement, identity transition, or uncertain/declined deletion cannot inherit the old extra-learning eligibility. These branches invalidate existing eligibility even if they preserve or restore equal text/taps. They do not merely refrain from setting a new ownership flag. A fully new own occurrence can qualify later; inherited text cannot become owned by callback, equal spelling, padding, or appending a suffix.

Useful minimal counterexamples and controls:

- Own `planet`, suffix backspace to `plane`, then emoji is a legitimate positive when lexical/readback premises hold. The exposed R44 case17 and Space57 match at five points; that observation does not retroactively resolve R44's withheld premise. R47 should declare this deletion premise now.
- Delete a separator after an earlier committed word: the derived word is inherited even if its spelling is identical to an earlier composing buffer. It must not teach again.
- In the exposed EN correction fixture, `teh` plus Space becomes `the `. Backspace follows the undo branch, restores the original `TypedWord`, and pins `teh`; `PersonalDictionary.pin/isKnown` can therefore make it lexically eligible before a later emoji. The old candidate's ownership bit is not cleared in this branch. Its count/validity/readback guards can accept those restored taps. R47 expressly excludes this history: lexical validity and array equality cannot override restoration provenance.
- A host declining backspace, switching document identity inside its primitive, or changing the suffix must invalidate this extra channel even if later optimistic state or host text happens to match again.

This is a branch-local extra-learning rule. Preserve the existing undo, rejection/pinning, deletion destination, text and ordinary learning behavior.

## 3. Blocking clarification: samples, net weight and positive references

The draft requires every eligible reference increment to be “nonzero and exactly expected available samples.” That is correct for declared unsaturated fixtures, but is not universally the unchanged learner's semantics. At per-key weight 300, `TouchModel.learn` forgets old evidence before adding a sample. A real accepted sample can have zero net total-weight increment while still updating offsets. Sparse points and normalization also make Character count an invalid general sample count.

Freeze this bounded replacement:

> Fixed positive witnesses, including each second word and separately typed second occurrence, use predeclared histories below per-key saturation and supported keyed mappings. Their exact expected net increments equal their available mapped samples and must be positive. Other histories compare full before/after touch state with both unchanged Space references under the existing update/forgetting rules. Zero or unavailable reference evidence never satisfies positive coverage. Saturation is not repaired by changing the learner, dropping a witness or substituting vocabulary after reveal.

No saturation test or new internal instrumentation is demanded for this round. Simply constrain the positive fixture histories before authorship and avoid declaring a false universal weight law. Prior training still must match exactly across routes.

## 4. Binding source implications of the otherwise acceptable contract

**Identity at the primitive.** Capture the occurrence's identity, require it immediately before insertion, and re-read live identity after the actual primitive before this new touch contribution. Compare with the occurrence identity, not an optimistic identifier possibly updated during a callback. A→B in `MockDocument.onWillEdit` with identical `music` text and no `documentDidChange` must add zero extra touch. Old candidate `insertText:474–512` lacks that final live comparison; inherited `insertDocumentText` only updates optimistic context. Nil→UUID and UUID→nil are observable changes too. Nil→nil is permitted with its explicit observation limit. Any transition actually observed while editing invalidates the old occurrence; an entirely unobserved A→B→A or identical same-ID replacement cannot be promised detectable. Keep this local to extra touch acceptance; R39 lifetime/destination work remains separate.

**Exact live Unicode readback.** The scalar-prefix test must operate on the actual resulting document split, not only the locally constructed `prior + output`. Old candidate additionally uses Swift `String ==` for live before/tail readback, which accepts canonical equivalence. Concrete control: own decomposed `cafe\u{0301}`, then have the insertion primitive normalize that prefix to precomposed `café` while appending the same emoji, without a callback or ID change. Use exact scalar comparisons for the live retained prefix and suffix required by this contract. Do not normalize or rewrite the host text to make it qualify.

**Actual end, tail and effective boundary.** Require complete raw before/after contexts and collapsed live selection before and after, not only optimistic `expectedSelection` or a nil-coalesced tail. A caret before `al`, `.com`, `_tag`, apostrophe, hyphen or another frozen connector cannot accept a fragment. Test an otherwise eligible same-history word so these negatives are not vacuous. Preserve the exact right suffix independently of touch eligibility.

Before fresh blind authorship, publish the small supported punctuation/tail-connector rule or a fixed capability supplement: “bounded punctuation” and “known connector” alone leave the same avoidable oracle ambiguity as R44. Bare U+2019, `.com`, punctuation-led tokens, bare `#/*`, letter suffixes and components extending the final grapheme must stay negative without an effective separator. A separately formed emoji grapheme, including supported variation/keycap/ZWJ forms, can qualify. Effective leading whitespace can establish a boundary even when the payload itself is letters. Empty input remains the existing early return. No universal technical-token exclusion or exhaustive new emoji validator is required.

**Eligibility, policy and correction ordering.** Capture kept-spelling eligibility before this action's word learning can validate it (`Suggester.isValid` observes the personal model). Preserve current `acceptPendingLearning` order and its independently justified correction-touch event; a correction offer alone is not an accepted correction. **Exactly once and skipped readback.** Consuming an external-boundary occurrence must prevent later emoji, Space and identical callbacks from teaching it again. A newly typed same-spelling occurrence is independent; do not suppress by spelling globally. Declined, queued, lagging, before-hidden, after-hidden or otherwise one-sided readback gains no new touch and has no later callback recovery. Test each unavailable side independently. Successful ordinary pending-correction learning and existing word/pair calls may still occur under their own baseline rules; a zero-extra-touch control is not a demand to erase those events.

The evaluator must exist, be adversarially checked, and pass independent frozen source/executor/evaluator review **before** fresh sourceblind authorship. Freeze the final oracle/input as well before one paired reveal. Required enforcement is:

- Actual declared EN/ES/BI resources and equal isolated initial model state; baseline/candidate assembly and immutable source/executor/resource bindings. Resetting a document does not reset learned state.

- Rowwise unchanged exact scalar text, Character caret/selection, word totals/watched counts, ordered pairs and policy against baseline at the same operation. Space supplies the touch reference; it does not replace the external route's existing word/pair/text oracle.

Retain process status/stderr; stale output cannot override a failed invocation.
